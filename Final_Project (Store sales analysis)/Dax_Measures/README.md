# DAX Measures — Rossmann Store Sales

## Summary of Work Done
Created DAX measures inside the `Dax Measures` table to support sales tracking, promotional performance, year-over-year comparisons, and store/customer metrics:

- **Sales & Growth Measures:** `Total Sales`, `Sales PY`, `YoY Sales Growth %`, and average sales measures.
- **Promotions & Holiday Measures:** `Promo Sales`, `Promo Lift %`, and school holiday measures.
- **Store & Customer Measures:** `Total Stores`, `Total Customers`, and `Open Store Days`.
