## Project Overview

---

## Detailed DAX Measures Breakdown (`Dax Measures` Table)
All analytical measures are organized inside the `Dax Measures` table using their full measure names:

### 1. Sales & Growth Measures
- **`Total Sales`**: Tracks overall store sales performance.
- **`Sales PY`**: Captures prior-year sales for period-over-period comparison.
- **`YoY Sales Growth %`**: Measures year-over-year percentage sales growth.
- **`Avg Sales per Store`**: Tracks average sales performance per store.

### 2. Promotions & Holiday Measures
- **`Promo Sales`**: Tracks sales generated during promotional periods.
- **`Non Promo Sales`**: Tracks sales generated during non-promotional periods.
- **`Promo Lift %`**: Evaluates the percentage lift driven by promotions.
- **`School Holiday Sales`**: Tracks sales generated during school holiday periods.
- **`Non School Holiday Sales`**: Tracks sales generated during non-school holiday periods.

### 3. Store & Customer Measures
- **`Total Stores`**: Tracks total store count in the analysis scope.
- **`Total Customers`**: Tracks customer volume across the dataset.
- **`Open Store Days`**: Tracks active days when stores were open.
